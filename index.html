Dear Frances,

Ang unexpected talaga ng simula natin. Sa araw na akala ko puro problema lang ang mangyayari, dumating ka at binago mo kung paano ko maaalala ang August 17. What started as an organic and completely unplanned encounter became something deeply special to me.

Habang mas nakikilala kita, mas lalo kong gustong makasama ka. I love our UP Diliman dates, Maginhawa coffee stops, night rides, Tomas Morato strolls, random pictures, kulit moments, and even the times na magkatabi lang tayo without doing anything special. I’ve realized that the moment doesn’t have to be extraordinary—because being with you already makes it special.

I notice and appreciate everything you do, especially the little things. ’Yung baon na inihanda mo para sa akin may seem simple, pero it meant so much because it showed me that you thought of me. I want you to know that your efforts never go unnoticed. I remember them, I value them, and I keep them close to my heart.

We’ve already told each other that we love one another, and I don’t want those words to remain unclear or without direction. I want you to know that my intentions with you are genuine and serious. Hindi lang kita gusto dahil masaya ang mga moments natin. I want to choose you even during the difficult, quiet, and ordinary days.

I want to be someone who makes you feel safe, heard, appreciated, and loved. I want to understand you more, support your dreams, celebrate your happiness, and stay beside you whenever things become difficult. I cannot promise that I’ll always be perfect, but I can promise to be honest, consistent, and willing to grow with you.

I don’t want to rush our story, but I also don’t want you to wonder what my intentions are. I want to love you properly and wholeheartedly. And if you allow me, I want to spend every day proving that you made the right choice. I want to become not only one of your sweetest memories, but the greatest love you’ll always feel safe coming home to.

I love you, Frances. I want to create more memories with you—but this time, with a clear promise and intention behind every one of them.

Kaya may isang bagay akong gustong itanong…

Yours,
Theejay
